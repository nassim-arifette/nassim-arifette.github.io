## The problem

Task Manager shows numbers without explaining them. A chatbot explains without measuring, and readily invents a cause. Most people end up with a "PC cleaner" that deletes things at random, or a forum thread that may not apply to their machine.

## What I built

PC Agent is a Windows desktop app (Tauri, with a Rust diagnostic core). You describe what you notice, such as "my PC is slow when I open Chrome" or "what can I delete on C:?". An NVIDIA Nemotron model served by Nebius Token Factory then investigates with about two dozen read-only tools: memory, processor, disks, NVIDIA GPUs, programs grouped by application, services, startup programs, event logs, drivers, Windows Update and storage.

- **Every cited figure is verified.** A number in the answer must match the measurement it cites, or the model is asked to fix it. Anything that still does not match is marked as unverified.
- **Read-only by default.** The model can only propose an action. It runs after your click, and its target is checked again just before.
- **Privacy you can see.** Toggles decide what leaves the PC, and *See what leaves* shows the exact message before it is sent.

Beyond chat, the app keeps seven days of local history (so "why was it slow around 2 pm?" can be answered from data), watches with Windows notifications, a daily report that runs even when the app is closed, and a storage explorer that deletes nothing.

## Context

Built for the Nebius × NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon, *Best Apps and Agents* track. The repository also includes an experimental Nemotron Nano 4B fine-tuned on Nebius AI Cloud to choose the next measurement. A Windows installer is available from the releases page.
