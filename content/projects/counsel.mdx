## The problem

When someone disputes an AI-assisted decision, a plausible explanation is insufficient: it must match the actual records and preserve their integrity.

## What I built

Counsel brings together a case assistant, an agenda, and a traceability view. Its demonstrations link decisions, human reviews, and subsequent actions through fingerprints and sealed receipts.

A Mistral integration drafts explanations from disclosed evidence. Numeric claims are checked against the underlying log before approval.

## Demonstration scope

Built for the LLM × Law Hackathon Paris #2, the demo uses fictional people, companies, and records. A receipt establishes record integrity and existence at a point in time; it does not establish that an AI decision was correct.
