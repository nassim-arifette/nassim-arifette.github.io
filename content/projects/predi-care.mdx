## Research-to-product prototype

We built PREDI-Care during the A.R.CA.D 2026 hackathon to explore how clinical, radiological, and biological information could be presented together when comparing rectal-cancer treatment scenarios.

## What the prototype demonstrates

The Streamlit application compares surgery and watch-and-wait scenarios, displays interactive risk and survival visualizations, and exports individual or cohort summaries. Its engine combines an optional medical LLM with a heuristic fallback.

## Funding

Following the A.R.CA.D hackathon, the foundation announced an allocation of **€100,000 to the project to begin prototyping**, confirmed to our team in April 2026.

## Current limits

This is a hackathon demonstration, not a clinically validated decision system. The fallback scores are heuristic and the SHAP-like explanations are simulated feature contributions. Real clinical use would require trained models, calibration, external validation, and medical oversight.
