## The project

PersonalLearn is the education project built by Team 14 at the Hi! PARIS 2025 hackathon.

The application also explores adaptive course delivery: uploaded material is broken into learning units, with a calibration quiz used to adapt the level.

## Scientific approach

We analyzed approximately 1.7 million records from PISA 2015, 2018, and 2022, using XGBoost for prediction and SHAP to interpret feature contributions.

The submitted model achieved a test R² of **0.79**, placing second jointly on that metric. The approximately 0.85 score reported for internal validation is a separate result.

## Award and resources

The team received the **Capgemini Best Scientific Approach Award**.

The public team repository contains the modeling notebook, scientific report, and business presentation. This award recognizes the scientific approach; it is not an overall first-place claim.
